Tools for Path of Exile

Free, fast tools for Path of Exile players — plan your atlas and run the Labyrinth without guesswork.

Atlas Passive Tree Planner

Plan your Path of Exile atlas passive tree in the browser. Allocate up to the full atlas point budget, undo and reset freely, and switch between Standard and Ruthless. The planner shows which league mechanics are guaranteed to spawn in your maps based on the nodes you take, so you can build a farming strategy before you spend a point.

Share any atlas tree with a single link, or import one from a friend or a build guide.
